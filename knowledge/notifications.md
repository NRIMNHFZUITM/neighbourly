## How do notifications work?
<!-- keywords: pemberitahuan, notifikasi, loceng, bell icon, red number, unread notifications, see notifications -->

1. The bell icon in the top-right corner shows a red number when you have unread notifications.
2. Tap the bell to see the latest ones. Tap a notification to open the report, listing or booking it's about. It's then marked as read.
3. Tap **Mark all as read** to clear them all, or **See all notifications** for the full list.

New notifications also pop up briefly at the top of the screen. You're notified when, for example, your report's status changes, someone requests or confirms food, a booking is approved or rejected, someone comments on your post, an event you're going to is cancelled, or there's a safety alert.

## How do I choose which notifications I get?
<!-- keywords: tetapan pemberitahuan, notification settings, turn off notifications, matikan pemberitahuan, mute -->

1. Open Privacy & settings (from the menu, or your avatar in the top-right corner).
2. Under **Notifications**, switch each type on or off: **Report updates**, **EcoFood requests and confirmations**, **Booking approvals and changes**, **Comments on my posts**, and **Safety and wildlife alerts**.
3. Changes save straight away.

Keep safety and wildlife alerts on if you can, so you hear about snakes, monkeys or other hazards near your home.

## Why didn't I get a notification?
<!-- keywords: tiada pemberitahuan, no notification, missing notification, tak dapat notifikasi -->

- Check that the notification type is switched on in Privacy & settings.
- Notifications refresh every 10 seconds while the app is open. This prototype doesn't send email or phone push notifications yet, so open the app to see what's new.
- Notifications for a report only go to the person who raised it and residents who tapped "Me too".
