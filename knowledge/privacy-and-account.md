## How do I change my privacy settings?
<!-- keywords: tukar tetapan privasi, privacy settings, change privacy, hide my name, sembunyi nama, tetapan -->

1. Open **Privacy & settings** from the menu (or your avatar in the top-right corner on a phone).
2. Under **What neighbours see**, switch on or off:
   - **Show me in the neighbour directory**: your name, block, bio and interests in the directory.
   - **Show my name on new reports**: off means neighbours see your block, not your name. You can still choose for each report.
   - **Show my name when I request food**: off means the giver sees your block, not your name.
3. Each change saves straight away and shows "Privacy updated".

Township Management can always see who you are, so they can follow up on your reports and bookings.

## What can other residents see about me?
<!-- keywords: siapa boleh lihat, maklumat peribadi, personal information, my details, visible to others -->

Other verified residents can see your public profile (name, block, neighbourhood, interests and bio), your community posts and your food listings. They can never see your phone number, email, unit number or exact address. Only you and Township Management can see those.

## Are my reports and food requests anonymous?
<!-- keywords: tanpa nama, anonymous by default, hidden name -->

Yes, by default. TownCare reports, comments on reports and food requests show "Resident in Block X" instead of your name. Facility bookings show other residents only that a slot is taken, never who booked it or why. Event RSVPs are only ever counted, never named. You can choose to show your name in Privacy & settings.

## How do I hide myself from the neighbour directory?
<!-- keywords: sembunyi dari direktori, hide from directory, remove from directory, unlisted -->

Go to **Privacy & settings** and switch off **Show me in the neighbour directory**. Your posts and food listings will still show your name, but you won't be listed or searchable in the directory.

## Does Neighbourly share my photo locations?
<!-- keywords: lokasi gambar, gps photo, exif, photo privacy -->

No. Phone photos often contain hidden GPS data that can reveal where they were taken. Neighbourly removes this location data in your browser before the photo is uploaded. Reports use only the pin you place on the map.

## What happens to what I type into the assistant?
<!-- keywords: privasi chat, chat privacy, assistant data, chat log -->

Email addresses, phone numbers and IC numbers are removed from your messages before they're logged or sent to the AI model. The conversation is kept only in your current browser tab. Please don't share personal details in chat anyway. For personal matters, ask the assistant to create a support ticket.

## How do I delete my account?
<!-- keywords: padam akaun, delete account, close account, tutup akaun, remove my data, padam data -->

Ask the assistant to create a support ticket asking for your account to be deleted. Township Management will remove your profile and personal details, in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).
