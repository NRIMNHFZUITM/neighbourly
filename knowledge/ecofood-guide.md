## How do I find food to collect?
<!-- keywords: cari makanan, makanan percuma, free food, browse food, available food, filter food, halal only -->

1. Open **EcoFood**. The **Available** tab lists food that neighbours and local shops are sharing right now.
2. Narrow the list with the filters: food type (Cooked food, Bread & pastries, Fruit & vegetables, Groceries & dry goods, Drinks or Other), **Shared by** (Everyone, Neighbours or Local shops) and **Halal** (Any or Halal only).
3. Each card shows the pickup time and place, the halal status, allergens and how many people have already asked.
4. Tap a card to see the details, the pickup point on a map and the **Request this food** button.

## How do I share surplus food?
<!-- keywords: kongsi makanan, kongsi lebihan makanan, post food, share food, how to share food, share surplus, give food, beri makanan, new listing, senarai baru, lebihan -->

1. Open **EcoFood** and tap **Share food**.
2. Add a photo of the food (optional, but it helps).
3. Fill in **What are you sharing?** (for example "Nasi lemak (6 packs)"), the **Type of food**, the **Quantity** and a rough **Weight (kg)**. A packed meal is about 0.4 kg; the weight is how the community's food saved is counted.
4. Choose the **Halal status** (required) and tick any dietary notes and allergens, such as vegetarian, nuts, dairy, egg, seafood or gluten.
5. Under **Pickup**, choose the **Day** (today, tomorrow or the day after), the time **From** and **Until**, and the **Best before** time.
6. Type a public **Pickup point**, such as your block's guardhouse or lobby, and drop the pin on the map. Never use your home address.
7. Add **Notes** if useful, such as ingredients or what to bring, then tap **Post listing**.

Neighbours can then request it, and you choose who gets it.

## How do I request food that a neighbour has shared?
<!-- keywords: minta makanan, ambil makanan, claim food, request food, get food, nak makanan -->

1. Open the listing and, if you like, add a note such as "I can come at 7 pm".
2. Tap **Request this food**. It now says "Request sent".
3. Wait for the giver to confirm. You get a notification either way.
4. Once confirmed, the listing is reserved for you and you see a 4-digit **pickup code**. It also appears under "For you" on Home.
5. Go to the pickup point during the pickup time and show the code. The giver then marks the food as collected.

## How do I choose who gets the food I shared?
<!-- keywords: pilih siapa, confirm request, terima permintaan, decline, tolak, giver, pemberi, requests on my listing -->

1. Open your listing. You'll find it under **EcoFood → My food**, or from "For you" on Home.
2. Under the requests, each neighbour is shown by block (or by name if they chose to show it), with their note.
3. Tap **Confirm** for the person you choose. The listing becomes Reserved and they receive the pickup code. Tap **Decline** for anyone you can't help; they're told politely.
4. At pickup, check that the code they show matches the one on your listing.
5. Tap **Mark as collected**. The food's weight is added to the community's food saved.

If nobody has collected it yet and you change your mind, tap **Remove listing**. Anyone waiting is notified.

## What is the pickup code for?
<!-- keywords: kod ambil, pickup code, 4 digit code, kod 4 digit, code -->

When a giver confirms your request, you both see the same 4-digit pickup code. Show it when you collect the food, and the giver checks it matches. This way nobody needs to swap phone numbers, and the food goes to the right person.

## How do I cancel a food request?
<!-- keywords: batal permintaan, cancel request, cannot make it, tak dapat ambil, tidak dapat datang -->

Open the listing:

- While you're still waiting, tap **Cancel request**.
- If it's already confirmed for you but you can't make it, tap **I can't make it, cancel**.

Please cancel as early as possible. The listing becomes available again for other neighbours, and the giver is notified.

## What do Available, Reserved, Collected and Expired mean?
<!-- keywords: status makanan, available, reserved, collected, expired, tamat tempoh, ditempah -->

- **Available**: anyone can request it.
- **Reserved**: the giver has confirmed one neighbour and is holding it for them.
- **Collected**: it has been picked up and counts towards the community's food saved.
- **Expired**: the pickup time passed without anyone collecting it.

## What is the My food tab?
<!-- keywords: makanan saya, my food, my listings, my requests, senarai saya -->

**EcoFood → My food** shows everything you're involved in: the food you've shared (with how many requests each has) and the food you've requested or are collecting. It's the quickest way to find a pickup code or to confirm requests.

## Will the giver see my name when I request food?
<!-- keywords: nama saya, privacy request, giver sees, siapa nampak -->

By default the giver sees "Resident in Block X" rather than your name. You can choose to show your name in Privacy & settings with **Show my name when I request food**. Exact addresses are never shared, which is why food is collected from a public pickup point.

## How is "food saved" calculated?
<!-- keywords: impak, impact page, food saved, makanan diselamatkan, kg saved, statistics, statistik -->

When a listing is marked collected, its estimated weight counts towards the community total. Open **EcoFood → Impact** to see:

- What City of Elmina has kept out of landfill this month and in total.
- Your own impact: food you gave, food you rescued, listings you shared and pickups you made.
- Food saved per week, split between neighbours and local shops, with a table view.
