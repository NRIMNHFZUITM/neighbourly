## What are the quiet hours?
<!-- keywords: waktu senyap, bising, noise complaint, loud music, noisy neighbour, noisy at night, jiran bising, loud party -->

Quiet hours are from 10:00 pm to 7:00 am every day. Please keep music, parties and loud work to a minimum during these hours. If noise is an ongoing problem, raise it with Township Management through a support ticket rather than a public post.

## When can I do renovation work?
<!-- keywords: ubah suai, renovate, renovation hours, kontraktor -->

Renovation work is allowed Monday to Saturday from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm. No noisy work on Sundays or public holidays. Contractors must register at the guardhouse, and renovation debris must be removed by your contractor, never left at the roadside or in household bins.

## What are the rules for pets?
<!-- keywords: anjing, kucing, haiwan peliharaan, dog, cat, pet -->

- Keep dogs on a leash in parks and common areas, and clean up after them.
- Pets are not allowed on playgrounds or in facilities, apart from assistance animals.
- Lost or found a pet? Post in the Community feed with a photo and the area it was last seen.

## What should I do about a break-in, theft or suspicious person?
<!-- keywords: pecah masuk, break in, broke into, theft, stolen, dicuri, curi, rompakan, robbery, crime, jenayah, intruder, suspicious person, orang mencurigakan, car break in, security -->

1. If a crime is happening now, or anyone is in danger, call **999** straight away. Don't confront anyone.
2. Tell the guardhouse at your neighbourhood entrance. Guards are on duty 24 hours a day and can check cameras and patrol the area.
3. Once you're safe, make a police report at the nearest police station if something was stolen or damaged (you'll need it for insurance).
4. You can warn neighbours with a **Safety** post in the Community feed, for example "Someone was trying car doors at Block F around 2 am". Describe what happened, but don't post photos of people, plate numbers or names.

For broken locks, gates, lights or CCTV in common areas, report them in TownCare under **Others** or **Streetlights** so they're fixed.

## What are the parking rules?

Park only in your own porch or marked bays. Double parking, parking on corners, and blocking driveways or fire lanes are not allowed. Visitor parking is available near each neighbourhood's guardhouse.

## Is open burning allowed?

No. Open burning of rubbish, leaves or garden waste is not allowed anywhere in the township, as it is a fire risk and harms air quality. Use the BBQ pit for barbecues, and arrange garden waste collection instead (see the waste and recycling guide).
