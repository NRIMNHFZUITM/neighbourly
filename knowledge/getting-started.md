## How do I create an account?
<!-- keywords: daftar akaun, buat akaun, sign up, register, new account, akaun baru, join -->

1. On the welcome page, tap **Log in**, then **Create an account** at the bottom of the login page.
2. Under "I'm joining as", choose **A resident** or **A local shop**.
3. Enter your full name (for a shop, the owner's name), your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
4. Tap **Create account**.
5. Next, verify that you live in, or run a shop in, City of Elmina (see "How do I verify that I live in City of Elmina?").

Only verified accounts can see the community, so nobody outside the township can read what neighbours post.

## How do I verify that I live in City of Elmina?
<!-- keywords: sahkan, pengesahan, verify residence, verify account, unit code, kod unit, welcome letter, surat, tenant, penyewa -->

Right after sign-up you'll see the verification page. There are two ways to verify:

**With a unit code (instant)**
1. Choose the **I have a unit code** tab.
2. Type the one-time code from your welcome letter, for example ELM-VC14-4821.
3. Tap **Verify my unit**. You're verified straight away and go on to set up your account.

**Without a code (approval)**
1. Choose the **Request approval** tab.
2. Pick your neighbourhood and block, and type your unit number.
3. Optionally add anything that helps, such as "Tenant since August".
4. Tap **Request approval**. Township Management checks your unit against their records, usually within one working day, and you get a notification when you're approved.

Your unit number is only ever visible to you and Township Management. Local shops verify differently: see "How do I register my shop?".

## My verification is still pending. What should I do?
<!-- keywords: menunggu pengesahan, pending approval, belum disahkan, waiting verification, not verified, tak dapat masuk -->

Approval requests are usually reviewed within one working day. While you wait, the verification page shows "Waiting for Township Management", and you'll get a notification as soon as you're approved. If it takes longer than two working days, visit the Township Management Office in Elmina Valley with proof of residence, such as your tenancy agreement or a utility bill.

In the demo, you can approve yourself: open the account menu, switch to Township Management, go to **Verifications** and tap **Approve**.

## What happens after I'm verified?
<!-- keywords: onboarding, setup, tetapan awal, first time, kali pertama, interests, minat -->

A short three-step setup follows:

1. **Interests**: pick what you care about, such as Food sharing, Safety, Kids & families, Pets or Gardening. This helps highlight relevant posts and neighbours.
2. **Notifications**: choose what to be told about, such as report updates, EcoFood, bookings, community comments and safety alerts.
3. **Privacy**: choose whether to appear in the neighbour directory and whether to show your name on reports and food requests. Anonymous is the default.

Tap **Next** to move through the steps and **Finish** at the end. You can change notifications and privacy any time in Privacy & settings.

## How do I log in or log out?
<!-- keywords: log masuk, log keluar, login, logout, sign in, sign out, keluar akaun -->

**Log in**: open the app, tap **Log in**, enter your email and password, and tap **Log in**. In this prototype you can also tap one of the demo accounts on the login page.

**Log out**: tap your avatar in the top-right corner and choose **Log out**. You can also log out at the bottom of Privacy & settings.

## I forgot my password. What can I do?
<!-- keywords: lupa kata laluan, forgot password, reset password, tukar kata laluan, change password -->

This prototype doesn't have a "Forgot password" link yet. All the demo accounts use the password **demo1234**. In the full version, you'll reset your password from a link sent to your email. Until then, Township Management can help: visit the office or create a support ticket from a signed-in device.

## How do I find my way around the app?
<!-- keywords: navigation, navigasi, menu, mana nak cari, where is, cari menu, sidebar, bottom bar, tab -->

- **On a computer or tablet**, the menu on the left has three groups: Neighbourhood (Home, Community, Places), Services (TownCare, EcoFood, Facilities) and Help (Assistant). Privacy & settings is at the bottom.
- **On a phone**, the bar at the bottom has Home, TownCare, EcoFood, Book and Chat. Tap your avatar in the top-right corner for Community, Places map, your public profile and Privacy & settings.
- The bell icon in the top-right corner shows your notifications.
- On a computer, the red **Ask Neighbourly** button in the bottom-right corner opens the assistant on any page.

## How do I switch demo accounts or reset the demo?
<!-- keywords: demo account, akaun demo, switch account, tukar akaun, reset demo, sample data -->

This prototype runs on sample data stored in your browser.

1. Tap your avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Under **Switch demo account**, choose Aisyah or Daniel (residents), Roti Sinar Bakery (a local shop) or Township Management (the admin).
3. To start over, choose **Reset demo data** in the same menu, or in Privacy & settings under Demo tools. This restores every sample report, listing and booking.
