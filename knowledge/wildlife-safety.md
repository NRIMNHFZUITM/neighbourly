## What should I do if monkeys come near my house?
<!-- keywords: monyet, kera, macaque, curi sampah, monkeys bins, monkey attack -->

Long-tailed macaques live in the green areas around City of Elmina. To keep them away:

1. Never feed them, even from a distance.
2. Keep bin lids closed, and don't leave food or rubbish bags outside.
3. Close windows and doors when food is out.

If a monkey comes close, stay calm, avoid eye contact and back away slowly. If monkeys keep coming back to an area, report a Wildlife sighting in TownCare so the Wildlife & Safety team can respond.

## What should I do if I see a snake?
<!-- keywords: ular, ular sawa, cobra, python, snake in house, snake garden -->

Keep your distance and keep children and pets away. Don't try to catch or kill it. If the snake is inside your home, or close to a playground or path, call 999 or the Fire and Rescue Department (Bomba) on 994. Then report it in TownCare as a Wildlife sighting so neighbours get an alert.

## Are monitor lizards dangerous?
<!-- keywords: biawak, lizard -->

Monitor lizards (biawak) are common near drains and lakes and usually avoid people. Leave them alone and give them space. Report one in TownCare if it is trapped or inside a building.

## What should I do if I see a wild boar?
<!-- keywords: babi hutan, boar -->

Don't approach or corner it, especially if it has young. Move away calmly and go indoors if needed. Report the sighting in TownCare so management can check the fencing near forest edges.

## How do wildlife alerts work?
<!-- keywords: amaran hidupan liar, wildlife alert, alert near me, amaran berdekatan -->

When a resident reports a Wildlife sighting in TownCare, everyone in that neighbourhood gets a "Wildlife alert near you" notification, and the alert appears at the top of Home until the report is resolved. Township Management can also post safety alerts for the whole township. You can turn alert notifications on or off in Privacy & settings under **Safety and wildlife alerts**.
