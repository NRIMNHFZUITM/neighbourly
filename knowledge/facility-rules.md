## What are the general rules for all facilities?

- Arrive and leave within your booked time, including setting up and cleaning up.
- Leave the space clean and take your rubbish with you or use the bins provided.
- No smoking or vaping in any facility.
- Keep noise reasonable and respect quiet hours after 10:00 pm.
- Report any damage through TownCare so it can be fixed for the next person.

## What are the rules for the multipurpose hall?

- Events must end by 11:00 pm, with the hall cleared by then.
- Decorations must not damage walls or ceilings: no nails, staples or glitter.
- Amplified music is allowed until 10:00 pm.
- The person who booked must stay for the whole event.

## What are the rules for the BBQ pit?

- Use the BBQ pit only. Open fires anywhere else in the park are not allowed.
- Bring your own charcoal and utensils, and make sure the fire is fully out before you leave.
- Keep children away from the pit while it is lit.
- Clear food scraps promptly, because uncovered food attracts monkeys.

## What are the rules for the gym and fitness studio?

- Wear proper sports attire and closed shoes.
- Residents under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.
- Wipe down equipment after use and return weights to the rack.

## What are the rules for the meeting room?

- Suitable for residents' association meetings, study groups and small workshops.
- No cooking. Light snacks and drinks are fine.
- Return the furniture to its original layout.
