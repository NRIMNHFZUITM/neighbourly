## When is the Township Management Office open?
<!-- keywords: pejabat pengurusan, pejabat dibuka, bila dibuka, buka pukul berapa, management office, office hours, waktu pejabat -->

The Township Management Office in Elmina Valley is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:30 pm, and Saturday 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Outside office hours, use the assistant to create a support ticket, or call 999 for emergencies.

## What are the facility opening hours?
<!-- keywords: waktu gym, waktu dewan, gym hours, hall hours, bbq hours, open on sunday, open today, buka hari ini, buka hari ahad, weekend, hujung minggu, gym open -->

- Multipurpose Hall: 8:00 am to 11:00 pm daily (bookings only).
- Gym & Fitness Studio: 6:00 am to 10:00 pm daily.
- Central Park BBQ Pit: 10:00 am to 11:00 pm daily.
- Elmina Gardens Meeting Room: 9:00 am to 10:00 pm daily.

## When is Elmina Valley Central Park open?

The park's jogging tracks and playgrounds are open from 6:00 am to 8:00 pm daily. The lake area and trails close at dusk for safety. The BBQ pit stays open for bookings until 11:00 pm.

## Are the guardhouses open all the time?

Yes. Guardhouses at each neighbourhood entrance are staffed 24 hours a day. Guards can help with visitor access and deliveries, but please don't leave food or parcels for neighbours at the guardhouse unless the guard has agreed.

## When can I drop off recycling?

The recycling drop-off point next to Elmina Lakeside Mall accepts sorted paper, plastic, cans and glass from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm daily. See the waste and recycling guide for what goes where.
