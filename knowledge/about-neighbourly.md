## What is Neighbourly?
<!-- keywords: apa itu neighbourly, what is neighbourly, neighbourly app, introduction, pengenalan, about the app, what does the app do, features, ciri-ciri, overview -->

Neighbourly is the private community app for verified residents and local shops of City of Elmina. Everything happens in one place:

1. **Home**: today's weather, safety alerts, things that need your attention and what's coming up.
2. **Community**: a neighbourhood feed with topics, community events with RSVPs, and a directory of neighbours.
3. **Places**: a map of every bookable facility and verified local shop.
4. **TownCare**: report township issues such as potholes, broken streetlights, rubbish or wildlife, and follow each fix from Open to Resolved.
5. **EcoFood**: share surplus food with neighbours, collect food that others share, and see how many kilograms the community has saved.
6. **Facilities**: book the multipurpose hall, gym studio, BBQ pit or meeting room.
7. **Assistant**: ask how to do anything in the app, or about township rules, opening hours and safety.

Only verified residents and approved shops can see what is posted. Township Management uses the reports and bookings to plan maintenance and services.

## Does Neighbourly cost anything?
<!-- keywords: percuma, bayar, free app, price, harga, subscription, langganan -->

No. Neighbourly is free for residents and local shops. Facility bookings follow each facility's own rules, and the facilities in this prototype are free to book. Shops can choose to share surplus food for free or at a discount.

## How do I contact Township Management?
<!-- keywords: hubungi pengurusan, contact management, speak to admin, cakap dengan pengurusan, complain, aduan pengurusan, office -->

1. Ask the assistant first. Most questions about the app, rules and opening hours are answered straight away.
2. If the assistant can't help, it offers **Create a support ticket**. Tap it, check the subject and details, and tap **Send ticket**. You get a reference number (for example NB-1043) and a notification when the team replies.
3. You can also visit the Township Management Office in Elmina Valley during office hours.

For maintenance problems such as potholes or broken lights, use TownCare instead, so the right team is assigned. For emergencies, always call 999.
