## How do I find and connect with neighbours?
<!-- keywords: cari jiran, find neighbours, neighbour directory, direktori jiran, meet neighbours, kenal jiran -->

1. Open **Community** and choose the **Neighbours** tab.
2. Search by name or shop name, or filter by **neighbourhood** and **interest** (for example Pets or Gardening).
3. Tap a person to open their public profile and see their bio, interests, posts and the food they've shared.

Only verified residents and local shops who chose to be listed appear. Phone numbers, emails and unit numbers are never shown.

## What is on my public profile?
<!-- keywords: profil awam, public profile, what others see, my profile, profil saya -->

Your public profile shows your name, block and neighbourhood, your bio and interests, your badges, your recent posts, the food you've shared, and only the reports you chose to sign with your name. Badges are earned automatically, such as **Shares surplus food**, **Township helper** (signed reports), **Community voice** (posts) and **New neighbour**.

To see it, tap your avatar in the top-right corner and choose **My public profile**.

## How do I edit my profile?
<!-- keywords: edit profil, ubah profil, change name, tukar nama, bio, about me, phone number -->

1. Open **Privacy & settings**.
2. Under **Profile**, change your **Name** or **About you** (shown on your public profile). **Phone** is private: only Township Management can see it.
3. Tap **Save profile**.

Your interests are chosen during setup. Changing them later isn't available in this version yet.

## Can I message a neighbour directly?
<!-- keywords: mesej jiran, message neighbour, private message, dm, chat, contact neighbour, hubungi jiran -->

There's no private messaging in this version, so nobody has to share their phone number. You can comment on a neighbour's post, add a note when you request their food, or meet at an event. Use a Community post if you're looking for someone, for example a lost pet's owner.
