## What kinds of food are allowed on EcoFood?
<!-- keywords: what can I share, allowed food, boleh kongsi apa, makanan yang boleh dikongsi, not allowed food, tidak boleh kongsi -->

You can share:

- Unopened packaged food that is within its expiry date.
- Fresh fruit and vegetables.
- Bread and pastries.
- Home-cooked or catered food that has been handled safely.

Please don't share opened drinks, food that has been partly eaten, raw meat or seafood that hasn't been kept chilled, alcohol, or anything past its expiry date.

## How do I share cooked food safely?
<!-- keywords: makanan dimasak, nasi lemak, lauk, leftover, cooked food safety, basi -->

- Share cooked food within 2 hours of cooking, or chill it within 2 hours and share it within 24 hours.
- Use clean, covered containers and set a realistic pickup window.
- Keep hot food hot and cold food cold until pickup.
- If in doubt, don't share it.

## How should I label halal status and allergens?
<!-- keywords: halal, tidak halal, non-halal, alahan, allergen, allergy label -->

Every listing has a required halal field: Halal, Non-halal, or Halal status unknown. Choose "Halal" only if all the ingredients and preparation are halal, for example food from a halal-certified shop or prepared in a halal kitchen. Also tick any allergens, such as nuts, dairy, egg, seafood or gluten, so neighbours can choose safely.

## What should I check when I collect food?

Check that the food looks and smells right, the packaging is intact, and the expiry date hasn't passed. Eat cooked food soon after collecting it. If you have allergies, ask the giver about ingredients by leaving a note with your request.
