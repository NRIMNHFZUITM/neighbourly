## What tools does Township Management have?
<!-- keywords: township management features, township management tools, what township management does, admin features, admin can do, apa admin boleh buat -->

Township Management accounts see a **Township Management** section in the menu with:

- **Dashboard**: key figures and charts for the whole township.
- **Reports**: assign, update and reply to TownCare reports.
- **Bookings**: approve hall requests and block slots for maintenance.
- **Verifications**: approve new residents and local shops.
- **Support tickets**: questions the assistant couldn't answer and requests from residents.

They can also post safety alerts that appear on residents' Home pages. Only Township Management can see residents' contact details.

## What does the township dashboard show?
<!-- keywords: papan pemuka, dashboard charts, statistics, statistik, kpi, analytics -->

The dashboard shows open reports, reports resolved in the last 30 days, the average time to resolve, food saved, active residents, bookings, wildlife sightings and open support tickets. Charts show new reports per week by category, average time to resolve by category, wildlife sightings per week, food saved per week and facility use. Every chart has a **Table** button for exact numbers. "Needs attention" lists waiting verifications, booking requests, tickets and the newest open reports.

## How does Township Management update a report?
<!-- keywords: kemas kini aduan, update report status, assign team, mark resolved, admin report, reply to report -->

1. Open **Reports** and click a report in the table.
2. In the panel, see the reporter's private contact details, the photo, the location and the history.
3. Under **Update status**, choose **Open**, **In progress** or **Resolved**, pick the **Assigned team** and add a **Note for the reporter**.
4. Click **Mark in progress & notify** (or **Mark resolved & notify**). The reporter and everyone who said "Me too" get a notification.
5. To answer publicly, type a **Public reply** and click **Post reply**. It appears on the report as an official comment.

## How does Township Management approve a hall booking?
<!-- keywords: lulus tempahan, approve booking, reject booking, tolak tempahan, booking requests, hall request -->

1. Open **Bookings**. Requests waiting for approval are listed first, with the resident's details and purpose.
2. Add a note to the resident if needed, such as "Please return the keys to the guardhouse".
3. Click **Approve** or **Reject**. The resident is notified straight away, with your note.

## How does Township Management block a facility for maintenance?
<!-- keywords: sekat kemudahan, block slot, maintenance block, tutup kemudahan, close facility -->

1. Open **Bookings** and find the **Maintenance** form.
2. Choose the **Facility**, **Date**, **Start** time and **Duration**, and give a **Reason**, such as "Grill cleaning & servicing".
3. Click **Block for maintenance**.

Residents then see those slots as Maintenance. Any resident bookings in that window are cancelled automatically, and those residents are notified.

## How does Township Management verify residents and shops?
<!-- keywords: sahkan penduduk, approve resident, verify shop, approve shop, pengesahan admin -->

1. Open **Verifications**. Residents without a unit code and new local shops wait here.
2. Check the details against your records: the unit for residents, and the SSM number and address for shops.
3. Click **Approve** or **Reject**. The person is notified. Approved shops appear on the Places map.

## How does Township Management post a safety alert?
<!-- keywords: hantar amaran, post alert, safety alert, amaran keselamatan, announcement, broadcast, water interruption -->

1. On the **Dashboard**, click **Post safety alert**.
2. Enter a **Title** (for example "Water interruption on Thursday") and a **Message**.
3. Under **Who should see it?**, choose everyone in City of Elmina or one neighbourhood.
4. Click **Post alert**. It appears at the top of residents' Home pages and is sent as a notification.

## How does Township Management handle support tickets?
<!-- keywords: urus tiket, handle tickets, resolve ticket, support tickets admin -->

Open **Support tickets** to see each ticket's reference, subject, details and the resident's contact details. Tickets that came from the assistant are labelled, so you can see which questions the guide should cover. Follow up with the resident, then click **Mark resolved**. The resident is notified that the ticket is closed.
