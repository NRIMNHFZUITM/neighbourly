## How do I register my shop?
<!-- keywords: daftar kedai, register shop, shop account, akaun kedai, business account, apply as shop, mohon kedai -->

1. Create an account and under "I'm joining as" choose **A local shop**.
2. On the next page, fill in the shop details: **Shop name**, **Type of business** (for example Bakery or Café), **Halal status**, **Opening hours** and **Shop address**.
3. Choose the **Nearest neighbourhood** and enter your **SSM registration no.**
4. Under **Shop location**, drag the pin to your shop. This is where it appears on the Places map.
5. Tap **Submit shop application**.

Township Management checks your SSM registration and approves the account, and you get a notification when it's done.

## What can a local shop do in Neighbourly?
<!-- keywords: kedai boleh buat apa, shop features, business benefits, shop can do -->

- Share end-of-day surplus on EcoFood, free or at a discount. Shop listings carry a "Local shop" badge and the shop's halal status.
- Appear on the **Places** map, with opening hours, address and how many listings are available now.
- Have a public shop profile in the neighbour directory, showing what you've shared.
- Post in the Community feed, for example to tell neighbours when you share surplus each day.

## How do I post surplus food as a shop?
<!-- keywords: kedai kongsi makanan, shop surplus, discount, diskaun, harga, price, end of day bread -->

1. Open **EcoFood** and tap **Share food**.
2. Fill in the listing as usual. The halal status and pickup point (your shop counter) are filled in from your shop profile.
3. In **Price**, leave "Free" or type a discount, such as "RM 3 per portion (usually RM 12)".
4. Tap **Post listing**. Confirm requests and mark them collected in the same way as residents.

Posting at the same time every day (for example 8:30 pm for unsold bread) helps regulars plan their pickup.

## Who can see my shop's SSM number?
<!-- keywords: nombor ssm, ssm privacy, registration number, siapa nampak ssm -->

Only Township Management, who use it to verify your business. It never appears on your public profile, on the Places map or on your listings. Your shop name, address, opening hours and halal status are public to verified residents.
