## When is household rubbish collected?
<!-- keywords: kutipan sampah, jadual sampah, rubbish day, garbage day, trash collection -->

Household waste is collected twice a week, and recyclables and bulky items on a separate day. The exact days depend on your street, so check the collection notice at your neighbourhood guardhouse. Put bins out the night before or early in the morning, with the lid closed so monkeys and stray animals can't get in.

## What can I recycle and where?

Sort recyclables into paper and cardboard, plastics (rinsed), aluminium and steel cans, and glass. Drop them at the recycling point next to Elmina Lakeside Mall (8:00 am to 8:00 pm daily). Food waste and soiled packaging are not recyclable.

## How do I get rid of bulky items or garden waste?

Bulky items such as old furniture and mattresses, and large amounts of garden waste, are collected on the bulky-waste day. Place them neatly outside your house the night before. For renovation debris, your contractor must arrange removal.

## How do I report illegal dumping or an overflowing bin?

Use TownCare and choose "Cleanliness & drains". Add a photo and pin the location. Please don't confront anyone dumping rubbish; just report it.

## How can I reduce food waste?

Share good food you can't finish on EcoFood before it spoils. Neighbours and local shops post surplus food every day, and the Food impact page shows how many kilograms the community has kept out of landfill.
