## How do I post in the Community feed?
<!-- keywords: buat post, hantar siaran, write post, new post, kongsi berita, share update, post something -->

1. Open **Community**. It opens on the **Feed** tab.
2. Tap the box at the top ("Share something with Elmina Valley…") and write your post.
3. Choose a topic: **General**, **Lost & found**, **Recommendations**, **Giveaways**, **Safety** or **Activities**.
4. Choose who can see it: your neighbourhood only, or **All of City of Elmina**.
5. Tap **Post**.

Your post shows your name, because posts are public within the verified community. Never include phone numbers, addresses or other people's personal details.

## How do I filter the feed by topic?
<!-- keywords: tapis topik, filter topic, lost and found, barang hilang, recommendations, cadangan, giveaways, percuma, official posts, my neighbourhood -->

1. Open **Community → Feed**.
2. Tap a topic chip: **All topics**, **Official** (announcements and alerts from Township Management), **General**, **Lost & found**, **Recommendations**, **Giveaways**, **Safety** or **Activities**.
3. Use the menu on the right to switch between **My neighbourhood** and **Whole township**.

The number of matching posts is shown above the list.

## How do I like or comment on a post?
<!-- keywords: suka post, komen post, like post, reply, balas, comment -->

- Tap the heart under a post to like it. Tap it again to remove your like.
- Tap the comments button (for example "3 comments") to open the comments, type in "Write a comment…" and tap **Post**.

The author gets a notification when you comment, if they've switched those notifications on.

## Can I edit or delete my post?
<!-- keywords: edit post, padam post, delete post, ubah siaran, remove post -->

Not in this version. If you need a post removed, for example a lost cat that's been found, ask the assistant to create a support ticket and Township Management will hide it. You can also add a comment such as "Found, thank you everyone!".

## How do I report an inappropriate post?
<!-- keywords: lapor post, report post, offensive, kasar, spam, inappropriate, tidak sesuai, harassment -->

Ask the assistant to create a support ticket that describes the post, or tell Township Management at the office. Moderators can hide posts that break the community guidelines. Please don't argue in the comments.

## What are the official posts in the feed?
<!-- keywords: pengumuman rasmi, official announcement, safety alert, amaran, township management post -->

Posts from Township Management carry an "Official" label. **Announcements** share news such as planned fogging or a water interruption, and **Safety alerts** warn about hazards such as monkeys near a block. Filter the feed by **Official** to see only these. The newest ones also appear at the top of Home.
