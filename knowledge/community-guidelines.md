## What kinds of posts are welcome in the Community feed?
<!-- keywords: boleh post apa, what to post, ideas, allowed posts, suitable posts -->

Share things that help your neighbours, and pick the topic that fits:

- **Lost & found**: pets, keys or parcels, with a photo and where they were last seen.
- **Recommendations**: ask for or suggest a tutor, aircon technician or swimming coach.
- **Giveaways**: things you no longer need, such as books, baby gear or furniture. (Share food on EcoFood instead.)
- **Safety**: tips and heads-ups, such as someone trying car doors at night. Report hazards in TownCare too.
- **Activities**: invitations to cycle, jog or play badminton together. For a proper event with RSVPs, create it in the Events tab.
- **General**: thank-yous, questions and friendly updates.

Posts can go to your own neighbourhood or to the whole township.

## What isn't allowed in the Community feed?
<!-- keywords: tidak dibenarkan, dilarang, not allowed, banned content, jual beli, selling -->

- Insults, harassment, or attacks on anyone's race, religion or background.
- Selling or advertising. Local shops can share surplus food through EcoFood instead.
- Other people's personal details, such as phone numbers, plate numbers or addresses.
- Misinformation or chain messages.

Township Management may hide posts that break these guidelines.
