## What does the Home page show?
<!-- keywords: laman utama, home screen, home page, halaman utama, what is on home -->

Home is a short summary of your day, from top to bottom:

1. **Weather** for City of Elmina right now and for the next few hours.
2. **Notices**: the newest safety or wildlife alert near you, and the latest announcement from Township Management. Tap one to see more.
3. **For you**: things that need your attention, such as food to pick up (with your pickup code), neighbours asking for your food, your next facility booking and your open reports.
4. **Coming up**: the next three community events, with a "Going" tick on the ones you've said you'll attend. Tap **All events** to see the rest.
5. **Summaries**: how much food is available to share, how many TownCare reports are open in your neighbourhood, and how many new community posts there are today. Tap a summary to open that section.

## Where can I check the weather?
<!-- keywords: cuaca, hujan, ramalan cuaca, weather forecast, will it rain, temperature, suhu, panas, hot, storm, ribut, umbrella, payung -->

The weather card at the top of Home shows the weather for City of Elmina:

- The temperature now, what it feels like, the humidity and the conditions (for example Cloudy or Rain).
- Today's high and low, and the chance of rain today.
- The next six hours, each with its temperature and chance of rain. Hours with a high chance of rain are highlighted.
- A tip when it matters, for example "Rain likely from 3:00 pm. Keep an umbrella handy." or a thunderstorm warning.

The weather comes from Open-Meteo and refreshes every 15 minutes. You can also ask the assistant "Will it rain today?".

## What is "For you" on the Home page?
<!-- keywords: untuk anda, for you, tasks, perlu buat, pending, pickup code, things to do -->

"For you" lists what needs your attention, up to four items:

- **Pick up**: food a neighbour has confirmed for you, with your 4-digit pickup code, the pickup time and the place.
- **Requests for your food**: neighbours who asked for something you shared. Tap it to choose who gets it.
- **Your next booking**: the facility, the date and time, and whether it's still waiting for approval.
- **Your open reports**: TownCare reports you raised that aren't resolved yet, with their current status.

Tap any item to open it. If nothing needs you, it says "You're all caught up".

## Why is there an alert at the top of my Home page?
<!-- keywords: amaran keselamatan, safety notice, wildlife alert, notis, announcement, pengumuman -->

Alerts appear when Township Management posts a safety notice, or when a neighbour reports a wildlife sighting near you, such as monkeys or a snake. Home shows the newest alert (and how many more there are) and the latest Township Management announcement, such as planned fogging or a water interruption. Tap the alert for details. Wildlife alerts disappear once the report is resolved.
