## Where are the facilities and local shops?
<!-- keywords: peta kemudahan, where is the hall, where is the gym, kedai berdekatan, nearby shops, location of bbq pit, di mana dewan, places map -->

1. Open **Places** (in the Neighbourhood menu, or from your avatar on a phone).
2. The map shows every bookable facility as a red pin and every verified local shop as a yellow pin. The legend in the corner explains the colours.
3. Tap **Facilities** or **Local shops** above the map to show only one kind, or **All places** for both.
4. Tap a pin, or a name in the list, to see its details: opening hours, address, capacity for facilities, and how many listings a shop is sharing on EcoFood right now.
5. From the details, tap **Check availability** to book a facility, **View shop** to open a shop's profile, or **Directions** to get there in Google Maps.

## Why isn't a shop on the Places map?
<!-- keywords: kedai tiada di peta, shop missing, not on map, add my shop -->

Only shops that Township Management has verified appear on the map. A new shop shows up as soon as its application is approved, at the location it pinned when applying. If you run a shop and its pin is in the wrong place, create a support ticket so Township Management can correct it.
