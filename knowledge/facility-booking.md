## How do I book a facility?
<!-- keywords: cara tempah, book facility, tempah kemudahan, booking steps, reserve, sewa, book gym, book bbq pit, book meeting room, tempah bbq -->

1. Open **Facilities** (**Book** on a phone) and tap **Check availability** on the facility you want.
2. Under **Choose a time**, pick a date from the strip. Each day shows how many slots are free.
3. Tap a free time slot. The legend shows what each colour means: **Free**, **Booked (no names shown)**, **Maintenance** and **Yours**.
4. Under **How long?**, choose the length. Each facility has a maximum, for example 2 hours for the gym studio.
5. Fill in **What's it for?** (for example "Family BBQ") and the number of **Guests**.
6. Tap **Book now**. For the multipurpose hall the button says **Request booking**, because it needs approval.

Instant bookings are confirmed straight away. Every booking appears in **My bookings**, and you get a notification when it's confirmed.

## How do I book the multipurpose hall?
<!-- keywords: tempah dewan, dewan serbaguna, sewa dewan, book hall, hall booking, reserve hall, majlis, kenduri, birthday party, parti hari jadi, wedding -->

1. Open **Facilities** and tap **Check availability** on **Elmina Valley Multipurpose Hall**.
2. Pick a date (up to 30 days ahead) and tap a free slot. Taken slots are greyed out.
3. Choose how long you need it (up to 6 hours), then enter the purpose and the number of guests (up to 150).
4. Tap **Request booking**.

The hall needs approval from Township Management, so your booking shows as **Awaiting approval** until it's confirmed. Requests are usually reviewed within two working days, and you're notified either way. Read the hall rules on the same page: events end by 11:00 pm and amplified music stops at 10:00 pm.

## Which facilities can I book?
<!-- keywords: kemudahan, senarai kemudahan, which facilities, list of facilities, facilities list, what facilities, capacity, kapasiti, what can I book -->

- **Elmina Valley Multipurpose Hall**: up to 150 guests, 8:00 am to 11:00 pm, up to 6 hours, needs approval.
- **Gym & Fitness Studio** at Elmina Green Clubhouse: up to 15 people, 6:00 am to 10:00 pm, up to 2 hours, confirmed instantly.
- **Central Park BBQ Pit** at Elmina Valley Central Park: up to 30 people, 10:00 am to 11:00 pm, up to 4 hours, confirmed instantly.
- **Elmina Gardens Meeting Room**: up to 12 people, 9:00 am to 10:00 pm, up to 3 hours, confirmed instantly.

Each facility page shows a photo, the amenities and the rules. The Places map shows where they are.

## Which bookings are confirmed instantly and which need approval?
<!-- keywords: terus sah, instant booking, need approval, perlu kelulusan, awaiting approval -->

The gym studio, BBQ pit and meeting room are confirmed as soon as you book ("Instant booking"). The multipurpose hall needs approval because it hosts larger events ("Needs approval"). My bookings always shows the current status.

## How do I see my bookings?
<!-- keywords: tempahan saya, my bookings, booking status, status tempahan, upcoming bookings -->

1. Open **Facilities** and tap **My bookings** (top right). Your next booking is also shown on the Facilities page and under "For you" on Home.
2. The **Upcoming** tab lists future bookings, and **Past** lists earlier ones.
3. Each booking shows its status: **Confirmed**, **Awaiting approval**, **Rejected** or **Cancelled**, plus any note from Township Management.

## How do I cancel a booking?
<!-- keywords: batal tempahan, cancel booking, tak jadi, cancel slot -->

1. Open **Facilities → My bookings**.
2. On the booking, tap **Cancel**.
3. Confirm with **Cancel booking**. The slot is freed straight away for neighbours.

Please cancel as early as possible so someone else can use the slot.

## How do I change the date or time of a booking?
<!-- keywords: tukar tarikh, tukar masa, reschedule booking, change booking, move booking, pindah tempahan -->

1. Open **Facilities → My bookings**.
2. On the booking, tap **Reschedule**.
3. Pick a new date and a free slot, and the length.
4. Tap **Move booking**. The app checks the new time is free before moving it, and your original slot is released.

## What if my booking request is rejected?
<!-- keywords: tempahan ditolak, booking rejected, why rejected, kenapa ditolak -->

You get a notification, and My bookings shows the booking as **Rejected** with a note from Township Management explaining why, for example that the hall is reserved for a township event. Pick another date and request again, or create a support ticket if you'd like to discuss it.

## Can other residents see my booking?
<!-- keywords: siapa nampak tempahan, booking privacy, who booked, nama penempah -->

Other residents only see that a time slot is taken. They can't see your name or what the booking is for. Only you and Township Management can see those details.

## How far ahead can I book?
<!-- keywords: berapa awal, advance booking, book in advance, how many days ahead -->

The gym studio, BBQ pit and meeting room can be booked up to 14 days ahead. The multipurpose hall can be booked up to 30 days ahead because events need more planning.

## Why is a facility showing "Maintenance"?
<!-- keywords: penyelenggaraan, maintenance, closed, ditutup, tutup sementara -->

Township Management blocks slots for cleaning or repairs. Those slots can't be booked. If a maintenance block replaces an existing booking, the people affected are notified, and a facility update explains what's happening.
