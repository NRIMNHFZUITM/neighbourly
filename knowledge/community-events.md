## How do I find upcoming community events?
<!-- keywords: acara akan datang, upcoming events, what's on, aktiviti komuniti, this weekend, hujung minggu, events near me -->

1. Open **Community** and choose the **Events** tab.
2. Upcoming events are listed in date order. Each one shows the date, the time, the place, who is organising it and how many neighbours are going.
3. Tap a category chip to filter: **Community**, **Sports & fitness**, **Kids & family**, **Nature & green**, **Food & festive** or **Talks & workshops**.

The next three events also appear under "Coming up" on Home. Events marked "Elmina Valley only" (or another neighbourhood) are for that neighbourhood's residents.

## How do I say I'm going to an event?
<!-- keywords: rsvp, hadir, saya pergi, join event, sertai acara, attend, going -->

1. Open **Community → Events**.
2. On the event, tap **I'm going**. The button changes to **Going**, and the number of people going goes up by one.
3. Changed your mind? Tap **Going** again to remove your RSVP.

Events you're going to show a "Going" tick on Home, and you're notified if the organiser cancels.

## How do I create a community event?
<!-- keywords: buat acara, buat acara komuniti, create event, create community event, organise event, anjur, anjur acara, host, adakan acara, new event -->

1. Open **Community → Events** and tap **Create event**.
2. Fill in **What's happening?** (the title) and choose a **Category**.
3. Pick the **Date**, and when it **Starts** and **Ends**.
4. Choose a **Venue**: one of the bookable facilities, or "Somewhere else". If you pick a facility, remember to book it for that time in Facilities too.
5. Type **Where exactly?** (for example "Central Park lake, near the jetty") and add **Details** such as what to bring.
6. Under **Who can see it**, choose all of City of Elmina or only your neighbourhood.
7. Tap **Create event**. It appears in the Events tab straight away, and you're counted as going.

## How do I cancel an event I organised?
<!-- keywords: batal acara, cancel event, postpone, tangguh -->

1. Open **Community → Events** and find your event. It shows "You" as the organiser.
2. Tap **Cancel event**, then confirm with **Cancel event** again.

Everyone who said they're going gets a notification. If you booked a facility for it, cancel that booking in My bookings as well.

## Who can see that I'm going to an event?
<!-- keywords: privacy rsvp, siapa hadir, who is going, attendees, senarai hadir -->

Nobody else can see your name on an event. Other residents only see the total number of people going, and the organiser sees the same count. The organiser's name is shown, so you know who is running it.
