## What can the assistant help with?
<!-- keywords: apa boleh tanya, what can you do, help me, how to use, cara guna, how does the app work, panduan -->

The assistant explains how to use every part of Neighbourly, step by step, and answers questions about living in City of Elmina. Try asking:

- "How do I report a broken streetlight?" or "How do I check my report's status?"
- "How do I share surplus food?" or "What is the pickup code?"
- "How do I book the BBQ pit?" or "How do I reschedule my booking?"
- "How do I create a community event?" or "How do I filter the feed by topic?"
- "Who can see my name on a report?" or "How do I hide from the directory?"
- "What are the quiet hours?", "When is the management office open?" or "What should I do if I see a snake?"

Answers include a link to the right page. The assistant only answers questions about Neighbourly and City of Elmina, so it will politely decline anything else.

## How do I create a support ticket?
<!-- keywords: buat tiket, create ticket, support ticket, aduan kepada pengurusan, talk to a person, cakap dengan orang, human help -->

1. Ask the assistant your question. If it can't answer, it shows **Create a support ticket**. You can also ask "I want to create a support ticket".
2. Tap **Create a support ticket**.
3. Check the **Subject** and add **Details**: what happened, where and when.
4. Tap **Send ticket**. You get a reference number, such as NB-1043.

Township Management sees your name and unit so they can follow up, but other residents can't see your ticket. You're notified when it's resolved.

## Which languages can I use with the assistant?
<!-- keywords: bahasa, bahasa melayu, malay, chinese, cina, tamil, english, language support -->

With the AI model switched on, you can ask in English, Bahasa Melayu, Chinese or Tamil, and the assistant replies in the same language. In guide-only mode it understands questions in English and Bahasa Melayu and answers from the English township guide.

## What do "AI" and "Guide only" mean on the assistant?
<!-- keywords: guide only, mod panduan, ai mode, offline, model, answer source -->

- **AI**: answers are written by an AI language model, using only the township guide for facts.
- **Guide only**: the AI model isn't available, so answers come straight from the township guide, word for word. They're still accurate, just less conversational.

Either way, check the linked page before acting on an answer, because this prototype uses sample township information.

## How do I start a new conversation with the assistant?
<!-- keywords: perbualan baru, new chat, clear chat, reset chat, padam chat -->

Tap the circular arrow at the top of the assistant to start over. Your conversation is kept only in the current browser tab, and closing the tab clears it.
