## How do I check the status of my reports?
<!-- keywords: status aduan saya, my reports, aduan saya, check report, semak aduan, track report, follow report -->

1. Open **TownCare**. It opens on the **My reports** tab, which lists the reports you raised and the ones you said "Me too" to.
2. Each card shows its status (Open, In progress or Resolved), the category and how many neighbours are affected.
3. Use the **Category** and **Status** chips to narrow the list.
4. Tap a report to see its **Progress** timeline, the team assigned and every note from Township Management.

You also get a notification each time the status of one of these reports changes.

## How do I see all the reports in the township?
<!-- keywords: semua aduan, all reports, other reports, reports near me, aduan berdekatan, lihat aduan -->

1. Open **TownCare** and choose the **All reports** tab.
2. Filter by category (Roads, Lights, Parks, Cleanliness, Wildlife or Others) and by status.
3. Tap any report to read the details and comments, or to say "Me too" if it affects you too.

Reporters appear as "Resident in Block X" unless they chose to show their name.

## How do I use the TownCare map?
<!-- keywords: peta aduan, reports map, pin, pins colour, warna pin, map view -->

1. Open **TownCare** and choose the **Map** tab.
2. Each pin is one report. The colour shows its status: amber for Open, blue for In progress and green for Resolved. The icon inside shows the category.
3. Use the status chips to show only some reports.
4. Tap a pin to see a summary, then **View details** to open the report.

## How do I comment on a report?
<!-- keywords: komen aduan, comment on report, add update, tambah maklumat, ask question about report -->

1. Open the report.
2. Scroll to **Comments** and type in "Add an update or ask a question…". For example: "Saw it again this morning, it's getting bigger."
3. Tap **Post**.

Your comment is shown with your block, not your name, unless you've chosen to show your name. Replies from Township Management are marked as official.

## Can I edit or delete a report?
<!-- keywords: edit aduan, padam aduan, delete report, change report, ubah aduan, cancel report, batal aduan -->

Not in this version. To correct or add details, post a comment on the report. If you reported something by mistake, or the problem has gone away, add a comment saying so and Township Management will close it. For anything else, ask the assistant to create a support ticket.
