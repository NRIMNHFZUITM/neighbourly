## How do I report an issue like a pothole or broken streetlight?
<!-- keywords: buat aduan, lapor, cara lapor, lampu jalan rosak, lubang jalan, report pothole, report streetlight, report problem, submit report, hantar aduan, new report, depan rumah, rosak depan rumah -->

1. Open **TownCare** and tap **Report an issue**.
2. Tap **Take or choose a photo** if you can. A photo helps the team find and fix the problem faster, and hidden location data is removed from it before upload.
3. Under "What kind of issue?", pick a category: **Roads & potholes**, **Streetlights**, **Parks & playgrounds**, **Cleanliness & drains**, **Wildlife sighting**, or **Others** for anything else.
4. Give it a short **Title** (for example "Pothole near the Block C junction") and describe what's happening.
5. Under "Where is it?", tap the map or drag the pin to the exact spot, or tap **Use my location**. You can add a location description such as "In front of Block C playground".
6. Leave **Show my name on this report** off to stay anonymous to neighbours, or switch it on.
7. Tap **Submit report**. It appears straight away under **My reports**, the first tab in TownCare, and Township Management is notified.

## What can I report under "Others"?
<!-- keywords: lain-lain, others category, other problem, masalah lain, broken gate, signboard, cctv, noise -->

Use **Others** for problems that don't fit the other categories, such as a broken barrier gate, damaged signage, a faulty CCTV camera or a broken bench outside a park. Reports in Others go to the Facilities team. For ongoing noise from a neighbour, a support ticket to Township Management is usually better than a public report.

## Will my neighbours see my name on a report?
<!-- keywords: tanpa nama, anonymous report, nama saya, my name, show my name, aduan tanpa nama -->

No, not unless you choose to. Reports are anonymous by default: other residents see "Resident in Block C · Elmina Valley" instead of your name. Township Management can always see who raised a report so they can follow up. You can switch on **Show my name on this report** for a single report, or change the default in Privacy & settings under "What neighbours see".

## What happens after I submit a report?
<!-- keywords: status aduan, progress, after report, in progress, resolved, selesai, dalam tindakan -->

Every report moves through three statuses:

1. **Open**: received and waiting to be assigned.
2. **In progress**: assigned to a team (for example Roads & Drainage or Electrical) and being worked on.
3. **Resolved**: fixed or closed, with a note from Township Management.

You get a notification each time the status changes, and the report page shows the full timeline with each update and note.

## What does "Me too" mean on a report?
<!-- keywords: saya juga, me too, same problem, masalah sama, duplicate, support report -->

If you've seen the same problem, open the existing report and tap **Me too, I've seen this** instead of creating a duplicate. Township Management sees how many residents are affected, which helps them prioritise, and you also get a notification whenever the status changes. Tap it again (it then says "You said me too") to remove your support.

## How long does it take to fix an issue?
<!-- keywords: berapa lama, how long, bila siap, when fixed, resolution time -->

It depends on the problem. Cleanliness and wildlife reports are usually handled within a day, streetlights within a few days, while road repairs can take longer if a contractor is needed. Township Management tracks the average time to resolve each category, and your report page always shows the latest status.

## Should I use TownCare for emergencies?
<!-- keywords: kecemasan, emergency, 999, polis, ambulans, bomba, fire, kebakaran, urgent, segera, accident, kemalangan -->

No. TownCare is for maintenance issues. For emergencies such as fire, accidents, injuries or crime in progress, call 999 immediately (or 994 for the Fire and Rescue Department). If a snake or other dangerous animal is inside your home, call 999 or 994 first, then report it in TownCare so neighbours are alerted.
